# Asian Productivity Organization (APO)

**Type:** intergovernmental body · **Country:** Asia–Pacific (Tokyo)

## What it is, and its interest
Publishes Productivity Insights guidance for factory leaders in SMEs. [S009, unchecked] Its interest is promoting productivity; its guidance is advice, not measured results.
