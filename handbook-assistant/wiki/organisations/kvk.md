# KVK (Kamer van Koophandel)

**Type:** public body · **Country:** Netherlands

## What it is, and its interest
The Netherlands Chamber of Commerce; it surveys entrepreneurs in its Omnibus panel and informs and advises them on the AI Act. [S004, unchecked] Its interest is informing businesses; its summaries of the law are not the legal text.
