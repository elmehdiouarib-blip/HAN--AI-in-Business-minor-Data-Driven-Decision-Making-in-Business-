# AI Act awareness

**Sub-questions:** E3, E2 · **Last updated:** 10 Oct 2026, by /wiki

## What we know
### Accepted
- Half of Dutch entrepreneurs do not know the EU AI Act, and only 7% are well informed (June 2025 survey). [C017, S004]
- 44% of Dutch SMEs with 10+ staff expect the AI Act to affect them, against 22% of smaller SMEs. [C018, S004]
- Four in ten Dutch entrepreneurs do not know whether the AI Act affects them. [C019, S004]
- The EU AI Act has been in force since 2024 and applies in full from 2026. [C041, S004]
- Users, not only developers, are responsible for correct use: they must check whether an application falls under the law, make agreements with suppliers, be transparent with customers and train staff. [C039, S004]
- For SMEs using generative AI, the AI Act usually means limited risk, with light obligations mainly about transparency. [C040, S004]

### Unchecked (not evidence yet)
- The AI Act sorts applications into four risk levels: minimal, limited, high and unacceptable. [S004, unchecked]

## How it fits together
Interpretation: firms of our reader's size expect the law to matter, but almost none have acted; knowing the user duties is a gap in itself. [C018, C039, S004]
