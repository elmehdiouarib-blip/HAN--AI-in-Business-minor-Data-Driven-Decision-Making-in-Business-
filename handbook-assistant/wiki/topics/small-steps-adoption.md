# Adopting AI in small steps

**Sub-questions:** E4, E5 · **Last updated:** 10 Oct 2026, by /wiki

## What we know
### Accepted
- Dutch SME manufacturers increasingly choose small, feasible steps instead of large-scale programmes. [C023, S005]
- Process innovation, and using AI with employees involved from the start, are the key. [C042, S005]

### Unchecked (not evidence yet)
- The FME research says this approach works, but that time is pressing. [S005, unchecked]

## How it fits together
Interpretation: this supports a small, reversible first trial with the planner involved from step 1, as on handbook page 2. [C023, C042]
