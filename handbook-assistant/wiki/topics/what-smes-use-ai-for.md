# What SMEs use AI for

**Sub-questions:** E4, E3 · **Last updated:** 10 Oct 2026, by /wiki

## What we know
### Accepted
- AI use among Dutch firms with 50–250 staff rose from 20% in 2023 to 45% in 2025. [C007, S002]
- 16% of Dutch industry firms used AI in 2025, up from 10% in 2024 and 7% in 2023. [C006, S002]
- 30.36% of medium-sized EU firms used AI in 2025, against 17% of small and 55.03% of large firms. [C014, S003]
- Of medium-sized EU firms that did not use AI, 22.26% had considered it (2025). [C013, S003]
- Of Dutch firms that use AI, 35% use it for marketing or sales. [C008, S002]
- 32% use it for administration or management tasks, such as risk assessment, selecting applicants, translation or planning. [C009, S002]
- 18% use it for production or service processes. [C010, S002]
- 4% use it for logistics, the least common purpose. [C011, S002]
- EU manufacturing firms that use AI use it mostly for marketing and sales (30.41%) and for administration or management (27.05%). [C015, S003]
- Among EU firms that use AI, 33.46% of large firms but 19.02% of small firms use it for production processes. [C016, S003]
- In the OECD's 2026 survey, take-up of off-the-shelf AI tools continued, but strategic, targeted and secure integration into operations remained uneven. [C025, S006]
- The APO names four AI strategies for SME manufacturers: predictive maintenance and visual quality inspection, digital twins, human-in-the-loop operations, and federated learning. [C038, S009]

### Unchecked (not evidence yet)
- In 2025, 19.95% of EU enterprises with 10 or more staff used at least one AI technology. [S003, unchecked]
- Text mining is the AI technology Dutch firms use most: 12% of firms in 2025. [S002, unchecked]

## How it fits together
Interpretation: SMEs mostly use ready-made AI in the office; factory uses are recommended more than they are reported. [C008, C015, C016, C038]

## Debates
- **Office or factory?** EU manufacturers use AI mostly for marketing and sales (30.41%) and administration (27.05%) [C015, S003] · the APO recommends factory uses such as predictive maintenance, visual inspection and digital twins [C038, S009]. What is done and what is advised differ.

## Open gaps
- Unknown: a documented, measured case of AI planning at a Dutch SME machine builder. Our searches on 10 Oct 2026 found none.
