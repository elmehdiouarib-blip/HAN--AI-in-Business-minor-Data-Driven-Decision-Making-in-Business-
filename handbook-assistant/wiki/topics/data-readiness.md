# Data readiness

**Sub-questions:** E3, I3, I4 · **Last updated:** 10 Oct 2026, by /wiki

## What we know
### Accepted
- In 80% of the Dutch SME manufacturers surveyed, the use of data and sensors is not yet developed enough. [C020, S005]
- 83% lack insight into which parts of their production process suit automation with AI. [C021, S005]
- Owners of SME manufacturers mainly need concrete examples, practical step-by-step plans and support with investment choices. [C022, S005]
- Comparable case: in two German build-to-order firms, sales estimated delivery times at the offer stage from experience, adding a large buffer. [C033, S008]
- Comparable case: their planning process gave a high risk that the delivery date first promised would not match manufacturing capacity. [C034, S008]
- Comparable case: the first firm's model used ERP data covering July 2019 to March 2022. [C035, S008]
- Comparable case: that data set held 16,361 orders with 58,344 manufacturing steps; the firm completes about 300 customer orders a week. [C036, S008]

### Unchecked (not evidence yet)
- None.

## How it fits together
Interpretation: owners ask for examples [C022, S005], but the evidence points to an earlier gap: most firms do not know their own data and processes well enough to apply an example [C020, C021, S005]. This is the main finding of handbook page 1.

## Debates
- None recorded.

## Open gaps
- Unknown: whether the partner firm records promised and actual delivery dates for every order.
- Unknown: how many orders a year a Dutch machine builder of 50–249 staff handles; likely far fewer than the comparable case.
