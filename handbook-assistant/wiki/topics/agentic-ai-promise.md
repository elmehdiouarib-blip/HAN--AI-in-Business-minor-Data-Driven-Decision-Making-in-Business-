# The agentic-AI promise

**Sub-questions:** E4 · **Last updated:** 10 Oct 2026, by /wiki

## What we know
### Accepted
- Vendor view (PayPal): AI agents can automate tasks such as reordering inventory, sending invoices, updating financial records and preparing compliance reports. [C026, S007]

### Unchecked (not evidence yet)
- The PayPal author says that in 2023-24, AI uptake in small firms grew by 72% on average across OECD countries. [S007, unchecked]
- The same author says that to be found by AI agents, SMEs will need to provide AI-accessible product data. [S007, unchecked]

*Left out:* the survey figures in the same post (C027, C028) were rejected by the tester as second-hand.

## How it fits together
Interpretation: a vendor's post shows that an idea exists, not that it works for an SME manufacturer. [C026, S007]

## Debates
- **Promise against practice.** The PayPal author writes that "agentic AI could be just what they need" [S007, unchecked] · the OECD's 2026 survey finds that deeper integration remains uneven [C025, S006], held back by time, maintenance costs and skills gaps [C024, S006].
