## Summary (by /wiki from the saved text; unchecked)
A non-representative survey of over 2,000 SMEs in 12 OECD countries. Off-the-shelf AI use keeps growing, but deeper integration is uneven. Time, maintenance costs and skills gaps hold firms back, and cyberattacks are common.
