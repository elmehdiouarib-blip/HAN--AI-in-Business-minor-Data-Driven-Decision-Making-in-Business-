## Summary (by /wiki from the saved text; unchecked)
Dutch firms' use of AI in 2025 by firm size, sector and purpose, and the reasons firms that considered AI give for not using it. Based on the CBS survey of ICT use in firms with 2 or more staff. 2025 figures are provisional.

## Accepted claims

- C003 (E3): 49% name privacy.
- C004 (E3): 42% name unclear legal consequences.
- C005 (E3): three quarters neither use nor considered AI.
- C006 (E3): 16% of industry firms used AI in 2025.
