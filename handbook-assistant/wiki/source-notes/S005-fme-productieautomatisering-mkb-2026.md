## Summary (by /wiki from the saved text; unchecked)
A survey of SME manufacturers, mainly in Brabant, on production automation and AI. Data and sensor use is weak, and most firms do not know where AI fits. Owners want examples and step-by-step plans. Small steps with staff involved work.
