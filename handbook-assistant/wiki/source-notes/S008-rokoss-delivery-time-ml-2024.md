## Summary (by /wiki from the saved text; unchecked)
Two German make-to-order firms (rubber seals and metal parts) used their ERP history to predict delivery dates with machine learning. Predicting at the quote stage cut the risk of late delivery but was less accurate than the planners' later estimate. The customer's desired date and knowledge of bottlenecks mattered most.
