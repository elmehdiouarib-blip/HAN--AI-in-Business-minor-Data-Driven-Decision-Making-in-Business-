# Candidate sources: E3, I3 · 27 Sep 2026 · /search

**E3** What do Dutch manufacturing SMEs report as their main barriers to using AI?
**I3** What does the firm know about its own delivery-date performance and planning data?

Note on I3: only the firm itself can answer I3, through the interview, and interview material never enters this project. The I3 candidates below are **comparable cases**. They show what planning data build-to-order firms need and why delivery dates slip. The chapter can use them to frame the questions the owner should ask about their own data.

Status: candidates only. No claims have been extracted. The developer decides which ones go on to /appraise.
